## Source-quality policy

Primary law/manual/order beats a general webpage.

Official department order beats a third-party summary.

An official audit report is useful evidence but may describe a specific case and must not automatically be generalized.

A contract/tender condition is specific to that contract unless it explicitly derives from a universal rule.

Existing software/RFP describes process/data and is not automatically law.
