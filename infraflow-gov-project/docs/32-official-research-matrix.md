## Critical finding

The Gujarat Engineering Works Manual 2020 explicitly says that R&BD/WRD officer powers for works are contained in Appendix XXIII of Engineering Works Manual Volume II. We have not verified a current primary copy of that delegation table. Therefore the engine will not manufacture the missing monetary authority thresholds.
