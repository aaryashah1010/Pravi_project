## Research conclusions

### Conclusion A

The system should not duplicate generic government workflow/document management.

### Conclusion B

The project-specific innovation is dependency-aware construction workflow, source-backed rule routing, root-blocker analysis, field evidence, and AI assistance.

### Conclusion C

Authority routing must be department/project/jurisdiction specific and source-backed.

### Conclusion D

A tender/contract condition should not automatically become a universal rule.

### Conclusion E

Government rules need effective-date/version handling.
