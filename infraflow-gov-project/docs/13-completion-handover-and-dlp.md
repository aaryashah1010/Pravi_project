# 13 — Completion, Handover and DLP

## Completion readiness

Possible checklist items:

- physical works complete;
- outstanding observations closed;
- final inspection complete;
- required measurements complete;
- final records uploaded;
- as-built documents received where applicable;
- completion certificate/record available where applicable;
- handover documentation complete.

The exact checklist must be project/contract specific.

## Handover

```text
Completion
   ↓
Final inspection
   ↓
Completion records
   ↓
Handover to operating organization
   ↓
Asset status = ACTIVE
```

## Asset model

Project and asset are distinct.

```text
Project: construction of a government school
        ↓
Asset: government school building
```

Project can close while asset continues to be operated and maintained.

## DLP

DLP should be sourced from the contract rather than treated as a universal Gujarat-wide duration.

Official Gujarat contract documents can contain DLP terms that are specific to that procurement/contract. The prototype must display the source contract and exact dates.

## DLP issue flow

```text
Defect reported
      ↓
Contractor notified
      ↓
Rectification task
      ↓
Verification
      ↓
Close defect
```

## Final closure

Close project only when configured closure conditions are satisfied and an authorized role records closure.
